Seasonal Spotlight

The Banarasi Kadwa Renaissance

Celebrating the sacred, laborious Kadwa weaving technique where each floral motif is individually engraved on the loom with pure silver zari.

⚜
Centuries of Devotion

Woven Thread by Thread on Century-Old Looms

Unlike modern printed or jacquard textiles, authentic Kadwa weaving leaves no loose threads at the back. It requires two master karigars working in synchronized cadence for up to 60 days to produce a single six-yard brocade.

  • ✦ Pure 24K Gold-electroplated Silver Zari Weft
  • ✦ Zero floating threads on reverse side
  • ✦ Certified Silk Mark Organization of India
