Patron Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential guidelines regarding bespoke tailoring, shipping timelines, and bridal consultations.

⚜
Do you offer custom sizing and bespoke blouse tailoring? +
Yes, absolutely. Every creation at VIRAASAT—from our everyday Chanderi kurtas to our monumental bridal lehengas—can be tailored to your precise measurements. We provide detailed measuring guides and offer one-on-one virtual fitting appointments via video call.
How long does it take to handcraft a bridal lehenga? +
Our signature bridal lehengas require between 4 to 8 weeks depending on the intricacy of the zardozi and Katan brocade weaving. For rush bridal orders, please connect directly with our concierge team to explore priority atelier slots.
Do you ship internationally to the USA, UK, UAE, and Canada? +
Yes, we dispatch worldwide through DHL Express and FedEx International Priority with complete real-time tracking and full transit insurance. Express international transit typically takes 4–7 business days following completion.
What is your policy regarding returns and fabric exchanges? +
Because all our items are hand-woven from delicate natural fibers and tailored in limited batches, we offer a 7-day complimentary sizing alteration service rather than standard returns. If a non-customized piece does not meet your expectations, our client care concierge will facilitate a seamless exchange.
