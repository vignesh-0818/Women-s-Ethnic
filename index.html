Our Heritage & Ethos

Where Generational Artistry Meets Modern Splendor

Born from a reverent passion for India's endangered textile crafts, VIRAASAT was established to preserve the legacy of ancient weaving clusters in Varanasi, Chanderi, Lucknow, and Jaipur.

"We do not design garments for a single season. We create wearable heirlooms passed tenderly from mothers to daughters."

Every motif etched onto our lehengas and kurtas is hand-drawn, every wooden adda frame is tended by generational karigars, and every silk filament is ethically sourced and organically dyed.

Pillars of Haute Couture

The Art of Boutique Excellence

Uncompromising dedication to authenticity, ethical artisan livelihoods, and ancestral textile perfection.

⚜
🧵

Pure Handloom Silks

Certified mulberry, Katan, Tussar, and Chanderi weaves sourced directly from weaver cooperatives across Varanasi and Madhya Pradesh.

✨

Hand Zardozi & Mukaish

Genuine metallic tilla threads, beaten silver mukaish, and Japanese cut-beads applied with millimeter precision on wooden Adda frames.

👑

Bespoke Master Tailoring

Every piece is customized with ergonomic kali grading, structured can-can drapes, and plush cotton slips for featherweight royal comfort.

🌿

Conscious Slow Fashion

Zero mass production. We produce in limited atelier editions with non-toxic botanical dyes, ensuring ethical wages for our karigars.
