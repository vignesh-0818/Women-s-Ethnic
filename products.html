100% Handloom & Silk Mark Authenticity Guarantee

We strictly denounce powerloom counterfeits and chemical polyester substitutes. Each VIRAASAT creation is certified by the Silk Mark Organization of India, woven with pure Mulberry, Katan, or Chanderi silk threads, and tested for real silver-gold metallic zari. We provide a Certificate of Provenance with every couture purchase.
