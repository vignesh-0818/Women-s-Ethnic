Institutional Transparency

Client Privacy & Data Ethics

Our unwavering commitment to safeguarding the personal records, bespoke sizing blueprints, and confidential transactions of our distinguished global patrons.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

A Sacred Trust Between Patron and Atelier

At VIRAASAT Haute Couture & Ateliers, privacy is not treated as a technical obligation—it is an integral pillar of luxury hospitality. For over a decade, royal families, celebrated dignitaries, and discerning patrons across 14 nations have entrusted us with their celebratory wardrobes. We treat your personal identity, photographic fittings, and postural data with sacred discretion.

1. Information We Collect

To deliver an authentic bespoke experience, we collect only the necessary details provided during consultation, browsing, and checkout:

  • Patron Identification: Full legal name, celebratory title, shipping address, contact telephone, and verified email address.
  • Bespoke Atelier Measurements: 28-point anatomical measurements, posture slope data, blouse cup preferences, and armhole circumference recorded during virtual or in-person master fittings.
  • Order History & Provenance: Certificates of authenticity, pure silk lot numbers, and custom embroidery sketches created for your trousseau.

2. PCI-DSS Encrypted Payments

We strictly do not store, log, or cache credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank account credentials on our internal servers. All electronic monetary settlements are routed via 256-bit SSL encrypted PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateway partners (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, UPI, and PayPal).

3. Confidentiality of Virtual Video Fittings

Virtual styling calls conducted with our Head of Bridal Couture or master drapers are private, unrecorded sessions. Any measurement screenshots taken with your explicit consent are deleted from local cache within 48 hours of dispatching the completed garment.

4. Zero Third-Party Commercial Data Sharing

VIRAASAT maintains a strict zero-resale policy. We do not monetize, rent, or distribute patron dossiers to advertising brokers, affiliate marketing syndicates, or social media profiling aggregators.

5. Data Custody Inquiries & Deletion Requests

Patrons hold the unconditional right to inspect, correct, or permanently expunge their measurement archives from our database. To initiate an inquiry, contact our Data Custodian at:

Data Governance Office: privacy@viraasat-ethnic.com
