Pricing Questions

Investment & Payment Guidelines

⚜
Do bridal prices include customized measurements and alterations? +
Yes, unconditionally. Every bridal and couture lehenga includes unlimited virtual sizing consultations, personalized cup sculpting, kali length adjustments, and complimentary lifetime waist alterations.
What is the payment schedule for bespoke bridal orders? +
For bespoke creations exceeding ₹50,000, we accept a 50% deposit upon initial design sign-off to procure raw silk foundations and commence loom weaving. The remaining 50% balance is settled upon final photo/video approval prior to insured worldwide dispatch.
Are there additional customs duties or international shipping charges? +
All orders within India receive complimentary insured express transit. For international orders (USA, UK, Canada, UAE, Singapore), express courier delivery via DHL is calculated at standard regional rates, and all export documents are prepared to minimize customs delays.
