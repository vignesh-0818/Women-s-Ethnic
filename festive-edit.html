Atelier Guidance

The Art of Festive Styling

Couture secrets from our Head Stylist to help you curate balanced, majestic festive looks.

⚜
💎

1. Polki & Antique Gold Harmonization

When wearing heavy zari brocades or deep velvet kalis, choose raw uncut Polki or Meenakari jewelry with green emerald beads to create regal color contrast.

🥻

2. The Weightless Cape Drape

Pin the top corners of your tissue or organza dupatta across both shoulders. This frees your hands completely for festive card games while framing your posture like a royal mantle.

✨

3. Post-Festivity Muslin Care

Never hang heavy lehengas on wire hangers, as the weight stretches waistbands. Wrap the garment in unbleached pure cotton muslin and store flat in a cedar chest.

Ancestral Mastery

The Four Sacred Embellishment Arts

Every festive motif at VIRAASAT is sculpted by hand without synthetic imitation machinery.

⚜
🪡

Kadwa Real Zari Weave

Independently engraved floral motifs in real silver-gilt thread. The back of the fabric remains clean and smooth against your skin without raw thread floats.

🧵

Hand Zardozi & Dabka

Coiled springs of copper wire electroplated in 24K gold, precisely sewn down with beads to form dimensional Mughal jaals on velvet.

🪞

Abhla Mirror Embroidery

Genuine reflective glass mirrors hand-framed in resham thread loops that capture flickering diwali oil lamps and dancefloor chandeliers.

⚜

Gota Patti Ribbon Applique

Gold and silver foil ribbons hand-cut into geometric motifs (tukdi) and sewn onto border hems for radiant movement and heirloom value.
